Privacy policy

This policy applies to the SpaceHub franchise website franchise.spacehubstore.com, part of spacehubstore.com. Data controller: Space Hub OÜ. Questions: support@spacehubstore.com

SpaceHub takes the privacy of visitors very seriously. This document answers the questions of how and why we collect information on our website and what we do with it hereafter.

Our clients are legal entities and individuals who need a storage unit rental service, and property owners and investors interested in a SpaceHub franchise. If our client is a legal entity, we process personal data of its representatives. If our client is an individual, we process his or her personal data.

We process personal data of website users by applying identification cookies, as described in section 3.

1. Personal data and person responsible for processing

We process as little personal data as possible for the conclusion and subsequent performance of contracts with our clients. Such data include:

  • personal data, such as: name, surname, personal identification number;
  • contact data, such as: address, telephone number, e-mail address;
  • information related to service provision, such as: data related to performance of contract, enquiries, complaints, information on payment transactions;
  • communication data, such as: data collected via e-mail and website chat rooms; data collected via social media; data transmitted via messages.

We process personal data of our corporate clients as authorized processor, and we process personal data of individuals as responsible processor. If client is a legal entity, we obtain personal data from it as from responsible processor of this personal data. We also process personal data of persons who have contacted us through the forms on our website.

Space Hub OÜ does not process any data from banks or any other confidential payment data. Payment of invoices and related data processing take place through the Stripe service (stripe.com), which undertakes to protect personal data processed related to payment pursuant to applicable personal data protection law.

We do not intentionally collect any personal information about children under the age of 13. If you suspect that your child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us so that we can delete it promptly.

2. Personal data processing rules

We process personal data based on expediency. We determine the purpose of personal data processing and assume it in our further activities. We collect only the data that is necessary for us to provide our services.

We only process personal data in a lawful, fair and transparent manner. We always have a legal basis for personal data processing. We disclose actions we take with personal data.

We retain accurate personal data and take physical, technical and organizational actions to ensure safety and confidentiality of the data we collect. We protect personal data against unlawful or unauthorized destruction, loss, alteration, disclosure, misappropriation or unauthorized access. Our employees are aware of and comply with data protection requirements.

We retain personal data for as long as necessary for the purpose of collection or as permitted under applicable administrative and legal regulations. We retain personal data relating to disputes until the claim becomes obsolete.

3. Application of cookies on website

Cookies are small text files stored on the visitor's device. They make the website more user-friendly and ensure its proper functioning. Any visitor may refuse or restrict the use of cookies in the privacy settings of the browser; this may affect how the website works.

We use cookies provided by third parties:

  • Google Analytics and Yandex Metrica cookies, to analyse how visitors interact with the website, which parts they visit and how long they stay, so that we can improve it;
  • Google Ads, Meta (Facebook, Instagram), LinkedIn and TikTok cookies and tags, to show our advertising and measure its effectiveness.

4. CCTV cameras

In order to protect lessees and their belongings, CCTV cameras are installed in SpaceHub facilities. Only authorized SpaceHub employees have access to cameras and recordings. Recordings are confidential and are not shared with anyone, except upon an official legal request, for example from law enforcement authorities, or where release is necessary for performance of a contract, for example if an insured event occurs.

5. Personal data protection breach

If you become aware of a data protection breach or are threatened with one, please notify us immediately at support@spacehubstore.com. We respond to notifications of possible safety breaches immediately.

6. Publication of data protection terms

Current data protection terms are published on this page and on spacehubstore.com. We may change them from time to time, so we recommend checking this page for updates.

7. Consent

By sending a form on this website you agree to the processing of your personal data for the purposes described in this policy. Consent to advertising measurement (section 8.3) is separate and optional.

8. Franchise enquiries and advertising partners

8.1. What we collect

When you send a form on this website we collect your name, phone number, city and your answers about the space or the calculation. We also record technical information about the visit: the page, advertising campaign tags (such as utm parameters and click identifiers of Google, Meta and LinkedIn), the referring website and analytics cookie identifiers.

8.2. Why and where

We use this data to contact you about the franchise, prepare a free assessment and understand which advertising brings enquiries. The data is stored in our CRM system (amoCRM) and in our Google Cloud project located in the European Union. Names and phone numbers are not stored in our analytics tables: we keep only an encrypted (SHA-256 hashed) version of the phone number there.

8.3. Advertising measurement, with your separate consent

Only if you tick the separate optional box in the form, we may share your phone number in hashed form, together with the advertising click identifier and the stage of your enquiry, with Google (Google Ads) and Meta (Facebook, Instagram). They use it only to match the enquiry with an advertising click and report the effectiveness of our campaigns. The form can be sent without this consent.

8.4. International transfers

Google, Meta and our CRM provider may process data outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States, under their own data protection safeguards.

8.5. Retention and your rights

We keep franchise enquiries for as long as we discuss a project with you and up to 3 years after the last contact, unless the law requires otherwise. You may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, and you may withdraw your consent to advertising measurement at any time by writing to support@spacehubstore.com. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.
